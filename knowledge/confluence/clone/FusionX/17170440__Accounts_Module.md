2.
 
Functionalities &amp; Features

▶ 
Core Functionalities:

Customer Management

Signature Upload: Upload customer signatures as part of onboarding.

Confirm Signature: Validate and confirm uploaded signatures before use.

Signature Inquiry: View or search customer signature records.

Account Management

Opening Account: Create new savings or current accounts for individual or corporate customers.

Activate Account: Approve or reject account creation under statuses like Pending, Rejected, Special Rate.

Manage Account:

Inquiries: Access various inquiries such as transactions, balances, reversals, standing orders, etc.

Lifecycle Actions: Update, block, deactivate, close, set dormant, or apply stop payments to accounts.

Account Confirmation List: Final approval lists for deactivation, blocking, reactivation, closing, dormant state, and stop payments.

Teller Management

Operations: Carry out standard teller transactions such as cash handling.

Bankers Cheque: Issue and approve banker&rsquo;s cheques.

Fund Transfer List: View and approve internal fund transfers.

Checker: Verify transactions that require dual authorization.

Non-Counter Deposit Confirmation: Approve deposits made via non-counter channels.

Final Action: Complete final validation for cash or cheque transactions.

In-House Cheque Clearance: Clear cheques between internal accounts.

In-House Cheque Cancellation Confirmation: Confirm in-house cheque cancellations.

Reprint Duplicate Slip: Reprint transaction receipts.

Miscellaneous Charge: Post and confirm ad hoc charges.

Statement

Single Printing: Print statements for a date range on accounts.

Bulk Printing: Generate statements for multiple accounts on schedule basis.

Statement Inquiry: View transaction history by account and period.

Card Management

Card Issuance: Enable ATM or debit card issuance and manage related details.

Passbook Management

Issue/Print/Reprint/Update Passbook: Manage issuance and updates of physical passbooks.

Passbook Inquiry: Search or review issued passbook data.

Cheque Purchase

Cheque Purchase Create/Approval: Register and approve cheques purchased from customers.

Cheque Book Management

Request, Issue, and Acknowledge: Handle requests, issuance, and customer acknowledgments.

Status Update &amp; Confirmation: Modify and confirm status changes.

Cheque Book Inquiry: Track cheque book status and history.

Fund Reservation Management

Fund Reservation Creation: Reserve funds against an account for future use.

Update/Confirm/Release: Manage updates, confirmations, and releases of reserved funds.

Inquiry: View reservation status and details.

Cheque Leaf Management

Request, Issue, and Inquiry: Manage individual cheque leaves for issuance and tracking.

Standing Order Management

Create, Confirm, Cancel: Configure recurring payments for transfers or bills.

Inquiry: Review current standing orders and their statuses.

Post-Dated Cheque (PDC) Management

PDC Creation/List/Clearance: Register and process cheques with future dates.

PDC Cancellation &amp; Confirmation: Cancel cheques by type and confirm status changes.

Overdraft Management

OD Request (Permanent/Temporary/Casual): Process and approve overdraft facilities.

OD Cancellation &amp; Limit Adjustments: Modify or cancel granted overdrafts.

Confirmation: Confirm approval or rejection of OD requests.

Overdraft Balance Write-Off

Create &amp; Confirm Write-Offs: Handle closure of unpaid OD balances.

Inquiry: Track all written-off overdrafts.

Transaction Reversal

Create &amp; Confirm Group Reversals: Reverse groups of transactions, typically in batch mode.

Transaction Management

Batch Creation &amp; Confirmation: Upload bulk transactions (e.g., salary, loan disbursement).

Batch Reversal: Reverse full batches if errors are found.

Waive Off Charges

Waive Off Charges: Remove service charges for specific accounts.

Confirmation &amp; Inquiry: Approve and audit waived transactions.

Inward Clearance

Batch Create/Update/Validation: Upload inward clearing batches and manage errors.

Exception Processing: Handle invalid entries within a batch.

Outward Clearance

Batch Create &amp; Confirmation: Upload and validate cheques sent to other banks.

File Upload &amp; Return Batches: Manage returned outward cheques via clearing house.

Cheque Transactions

Receive &amp; Realize Cheques: Accept and process cheque deposits into accounts.

ATM Transactions

Manual Transactions: Reverse or confirm failed ATM operations.

Recovery Management

Recovery Status Confirmation: Update and confirm recovery status for overdue accounts.

General Reports

Comprehensive Reporting: Generate reports on accounts, cheques, reversals, charges, overdrafts, dormant balances, etc.

Kiosk Reports

ATM &amp; Card Reports: View reports on card requests, activations, replacements, and ATM activity.

Product &amp; Settings Configurations

Core/Main/Sub Product Management: Define and activate banking products.

Interest Templates: Configure rules for credit, overdraft, and bonus interest.

Fee &amp; Tax Settings: Define fee types, tariff groups, charge rates, and tax rules.

Eligibility Templates: Enforce criteria such as ID, age, residency, and officer tagging.

Business Settings: Configure document checklists, rate limits, notification methods.

Transaction &amp; Cheque Book Settings: Set codes, references, clearing types, cheque stock, etc.

Common Module Settings: Define frequency, product segments, event mapping, and automation.
